3D Scanning Services in India

VShark Solutions Pvt Ltd provides 3D scanning services across India from Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. How a job works, anywhere in India

  1. You send a photo, the approximate size and the output you need (mesh, CAD or report).
  2. We confirm the method and send a written quote.
  3. We scan at our Coimbatore facility, or travel to your plant. Travel and accommodation for on-site work are quoted separately.
  4. We clean the scan data and build the mesh, CAD model or report.
  5. You receive the files and we answer questions on them.

If the part is small, you can also send it to us. Larger parts, heavy dies and fixed machines are scanned where they stand.

How accurate is 3D scanning?

Accuracy depends on the scanner, the part and the set-up. The JINUOSH Vertex handheld blue-laser scanners we distribute are specified by the maker at up to 0.020 mm (Vertex3D and Vertex3D Pro) and 0.025 mm (Vertex Tracker). That is a scanner specification, not a guarantee for every part. We tell you the expected accuracy for your part before we start. See the JINUOSH Vertex scanners or how they compare in JINUOSH Vertex vs Creaform, Shining 3D and GOM. For the numbers by model, read handheld 3D scanner accuracy.

Industries

Scanning and reverse engineering are used in automotive, rail, aerospace, energy, mould and die, and machinery manufacturing (industries listed by the scanner maker for this equipment). Tell us your part and we will say how the job would run.

What decides the cost of a 3D scan?

We quote per job and do not publish a price list. The main drivers are:

  • the size and number of parts,
  • the surface (shiny, dark or fine detail takes longer),
  • the accuracy you need,
  • the output (a mesh is quicker than a full CAD model),
  • on-site work, travel and accommodation.
