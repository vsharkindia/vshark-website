Scan to CAD for Teams That Own a Scanner but Have No CAD Team

You have the scan. We build the CAD. VShark Solutions rebuilds your STL, PLY or OBJ scan as an editable, parametric STEP model, not an automatic mesh conversion, and checks the model against your scan with a deviation report. We do scanning and scan-to-CAD in India and the UAE, and we serve overseas customers remotely. NDA is signed on request before you send any data. Send your scan or a photo of your part for a written quote.

Who is this for?

You own a handheld or tracked 3D scanner, or your customers send you scans, and the CAD step is the bottleneck. Typical teams:

  • Automotive aftermarket and tuners: body kits, trim, ducts, mounts and brackets built around a scanned vehicle.
  • R&D and prototyping: a scanned part or fixture that must become a model you can change.
  • Small manufacturers and workshops: worn or obsolete parts with no drawing.
  • Restoration: one-off parts rebuilt from a scanned original.
  • Product designers: an existing product scanned as the base for a redesign.

If you only have the part and no scan, we can scan it in India or the UAE. See 3D scanning services in India.

Parametric rebuild, not mesh conversion. An automatic conversion wraps surfaces around the triangles and copies scan noise and wear. A rebuild decides which surface is a plane, which hole is round and where the datum is, so you can edit dimensions later. See also scan to CAD in SolidWorks.

How does the process work?

  1. You send the scan or a photo. A full-resolution mesh is best. A photo of the part is enough to start a quote.
  2. NDA first if you need one. Tell us before you send data and we can sign your NDA.
  3. Written quote. We read the data, ask the few questions that matter (purpose, which features must be exact, mating parts) and send a written quote.
  4. Optional small-part pilot. If you want to see our work on your data before a larger job, we can do a small-part pilot. Scope and price are agreed in writing before work starts.
  5. Rebuild. We set the datum frame, build sections and features, and model critical interfaces exactly while the rest follows the scan.
  6. Deviation check. We compare the model with your scan and report the result against the agreed target.
  7. Delivery. STEP files and the report go to you; you keep your customer.

What should I send?

The fewer things we have to guess, the fewer revision rounds you need. Send:

  • the scan files (STL, PLY or OBJ, full resolution) and the units;
  • the purpose of the model and which features must be exact, with any tolerances;
  • the material and process (cast, machined, printed), if known;
  • the mating parts, or a photo of where the part fits;
  • the deliverables you want and your deadline.

If you do not know an item, write "not known" and send a photo; we will tell you what to measure.

FAQ

Common questions.

What do you need from me to start?

Send your scan (STL, PLY or OBJ, full resolution) or a photo of the part, plus what it is for. Short answers are fine.

Is this mesh-to-CAD conversion?

No. Automatic conversion wraps surfaces around triangles. We rebuild the part with real planes, cylinders and radii so you can edit it. Where a surface is organic and has no clean geometry, it can follow the scan.

Which files do I get back?

STEP as the base, IGES or Parasolid on request, a deviation report and notes on what to verify. Neutral formats open in SolidWorks, Fusion and other CAD. Your original scan is returned untouched.

How accurate is the CAD model?

The target is set per job against your scan and the process (cast, machined, printed). We check the model against your scan and report the deviation. Raw cast surfaces and scan noise limit how closely any model can match, so we agree the target before work starts.

Do you work under NDA, and who owns the model?

We can sign your NDA if you need one; tell us before you send data. The CAD files we deliver are yours.

What are your payment terms?

50% advance and 50% before delivery. We quote in writing before work starts.

How long does it take and how is it priced?

Turnaround is set per part after we see the data. We quote in writing before work starts, so you know the scope before you commit. We do not publish price lists.

Do you work with my scanner brand?

We work from the scan files, so the brand rarely matters. Send the original, full-resolution mesh and tell us the scan noise you saw. If you have no scan, we can scan the part in India or the UAE.

Can I start with a small job first?

Yes. Send a quote request and tell us the part. We can do a small-part pilot on a first part so you see our work on your data. The scope and price are agreed in writing before work starts.
