### What to bring or send
- The part itself, and any mating part it fits with.
- Any drawing, old CAD file, photo or sample, even if it is only partly right.
- A note on what the part does, where it wears or fails, and the output you need (editable STEP CAD, a mesh, or an inspection report).
- The surface condition: shiny, dark, oily or coated surfaces and deep holes can change the scanning method. Tell us before you bring it.
