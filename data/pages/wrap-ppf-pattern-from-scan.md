### What you get
- 2D flat pattern files (DXF, PDF at full size) for each panel you ask for, ready for your plotter or cutter.
- Marked areas where the surface is too curved to lie flat: relief cuts, stretch zones and seam suggestions.
- A deviation view: how far each flat pattern is from the scanned surface, so you see where it will be tight.
- The scanned panel as a mesh or CAD surface on request, for your own design work.

### Who is this for?
- Wrap and PPF studios whose pattern software has no entry for a new launch, a facelift or a rare model.
- Interior-trim wrap shops working on dashboards, door panels, centre consoles and bezels.
- Custom bike shops wrapping tanks, fairings and mudguards, and PPF installers protecting them.
- Studios that cut their own pre-cut PPF kits and want a pattern for a panel nobody sells.
- Commercial wrap shops with an odd body shape: a custom van body, a trailer or a one-off build.

### The problems we hear, in your words
- **"The PPF pattern is not available for my car."** The database has the old model, not the new one, or the other trim.
- **"No wrap template for the new model."** Nobody has sold one yet, and the customer will not wait.
- **"The dashboard and interior trims have no pattern."** Interior parts have complex curves, and most pattern lists cover the outside body only.
- **"The pattern was close but not right."** An edge lifts, a seam lands on a character line, or the film is short at a corner.
- **"We template by hand on the car."** It takes a long time, there is a risk of scratches, and the next car of the same model starts again.
- **"Wrong cuts waste film."** One mis-cut panel on an expensive roll hurts, and re-cuts add up.

### How it works
1. **Tell us the model and the panels.** Model, year, variant, and which panels or trims you want. Photos help us say what a scan can reach.
2. **We confirm what can be captured.** Black or glossy glass, chrome, very dark gloss paint, deep recesses and tight gaps can need preparation or may not be fully captured. We tell you before we start.
3. **Scan the panel or the vehicle.** The whole car or only the zone you need, or a loose trim part. The vehicle or part comes to us, or ask us about on-site options.
4. **Unroll the surface into flat patterns.** We split each panel along sensible seams, unroll it flat, and mark where it cannot lie flat without a relief cut or a stretch.
5. **Check the pattern against the scan.** A deviation view shows where the flat pattern stretches or compresses against the real surface, so you see the tight spots before you cut.
6. **Deliver, then you cut.** You get the files, your plotter cuts, and your installer fits. We do not cut or install the film.

### What we need from you
- Vehicle model, year and variant (and market, if the trim differs).
- The list of panels or trims you want, and which film you use (gloss, matte, textured, PPF) if it matters to the stretch.
- Access to the vehicle or the loose part for scanning.
- Your cutter or plotter format (DXF, PDF, or your software's import format) and the sheet or roll width.
- Any seam rules you follow, such as no seams on a flat visible face.
- How many vehicles of this model you expect to wrap, so we can set the patterns up for repeat use.

### What we can and cannot do
**We can:**
- Scan a vehicle panel, an interior trim or a loose part, and unroll its surface into flat patterns.
- Mark relief cuts, stretch areas and seam suggestions, and show a deviation view against the scan.
- Provide patterns for a model, trim or bike that is not in your pattern software.
- Do this for a car, a bike or a one-off body, in India and the UAE.

**We cannot, or will not claim to:**
- Make a compound curve lie flat. A surface curved in two directions cannot be flattened without stretch or a relief cut. We show where, and the installer's heat and stretch finish it.
- Predict how your film behaves. Stretch limits depend on the film, its temperature and the installer. We give geometry, not a film guarantee.
- Capture every surface. Black or glossy glass, chrome, deep recesses and tight gaps can be missed or need preparation. We list what was not captured.
- Promise that the first cut will fit. Treat the first panel as a check, and tell us what you see so we can adjust the pattern.
- Cut or install the film, or take on a warranty for an installation. Your shop does that.
- Promise a tolerance for a whole vehicle. We agree what matters for your panels and report what we measured.

### FAQ
**There is no PPF pattern for my car in my software. What can I do?**
Have the panels scanned and unrolled into flat patterns. We scan the panel, unroll the surface, mark relief cuts and stretch areas, and give you DXF or PDF files at full size for your plotter. Send the model, year and the panels you need.

**Can you make patterns for dashboard and interior trims?**
Yes, if the part can be scanned. Interior parts have tight curves and recesses, so we tell you which areas need relief cuts or may be hard to capture. Send photos of the trim and the model.

**Do you make patterns for bikes?**
Yes, for tanks, fairings, mudguards and similar parts that can be scanned. Send the model and the panels you want.

**Which file formats do you give?**
DXF and PDF at full size, one pattern per panel. Tell us your plotter or software and the roll width, and we set up the files to match. Mesh or CAD surface of the panel is available on request.

**Will the flat pattern fit perfectly?**
A flat pattern from a curved panel is never perfect where the surface curves in two directions. We show those areas and mark relief cuts and stretch. How the film behaves depends on the film and your installer. Treat the first panel as a check and tell us what you see.

**Do you cut or install the film?**
No. We give you the pattern files. Your shop cuts and fits the film, and we do not take on installation work or film warranty.

**Do you scan the whole vehicle or only the panels?**
Either. Often only the panels or trims you need are scanned. Tell us what you want and we say which zones to scan.

**How much does it cost, and how long does it take?**
It depends on the number of panels, their complexity and where the scanning is done, so we quote per job. Send the model, year and panels and we reply with a written quote and a time after seeing your brief.
