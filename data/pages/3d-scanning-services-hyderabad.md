### FAQ
**Do you have an office in Hyderabad?**
No. We serve Hyderabad from Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, by courier-in parts, remote scan-to-CAD and on-site options on request.

**Can you do scan to CAD without visiting Hyderabad?**
Yes, for parts you can send. We scan the part in Coimbatore, build the CAD model and send you the files, with a deviation check against the scan.

**How should I pack a part for scanning?**
Use a rigid box with foam, keep mating parts together, and tell us if the surface is shiny, dark or oily, because that can change the method.

**Can you visit a Hyderabad plant?**
Ask us about on-site options. Send a photo of the part in place and its size, and we reply on whether a visit suits the job.

**How do I check the CAD against my part?**
We provide a deviation report comparing the model with the scan. Reports are normally delivered in 2 to 3 days.

**Which CAD formats do you deliver?**
Editable STEP, with IGES or Parasolid on request, plus the mesh.

**What does it cost?**
We quote per job in writing after a photo. There is no public price list.

**Which scanners do you use?**
We distribute JINUOSH Vertex handheld scanners in India and use the Vertex and Vertex Tracker.
