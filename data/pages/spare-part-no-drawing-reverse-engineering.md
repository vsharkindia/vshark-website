### When this is the right service

- The OEM is gone, the part is obsolete, or the supplier will not share the drawing.
- The part is worn, broken or deformed and you need the original shape back.
- A casting, forging or moulded part exists but its pattern or tool data is lost.
- You need a drawing for a machine that has run for years without one.
- You want a made part compared with the original to see what changed.

### How we rebuild a spare part with no drawing

1. **Brief and part review.** You send a photo, the approximate size, the material if you know it and the job the part does (what it fits into, what loads it takes). If you have the mating part, send it too: it tells us what really matters on the part.
2. **3D scan.** We scan the part at our Coimbatore facility, or where it stands at your plant. Handheld blue-laser scanning suits small to medium parts, castings and tooling; optical-tracking scanning suits large parts. Shiny or dark surfaces and deep holes can change the method, so tell us the surface.
3. **Mesh cleaning.** The scan is merged, cleaned and checked for gaps, so the mesh matches the part. The mesh can be delivered as STL or PLY.
4. **CAD rebuild and tolerance.** We rebuild the part as a clean, editable CAD model from sections and features of the scan, not as a copy of the rough mesh. On a worn part we rebuild the intended shape, not the wear. Fits, tolerances and mating features are agreed with you, because the scan shows the part as it is today and not what the designer intended. We do not promise a tolerance until we have seen the part and its job.
5. **Inspection against the scan.** We compare the finished CAD with the scan and show the difference as a deviation colour map, so you see where the model sits on the scan before anything is made. Inspection reports are normally delivered in 2 to 3 days.
6. **Delivery.** You receive STEP (also IGES or Parasolid on request) that opens in your own CAD software, plus the mesh and the deviation report if you want them. A 2D drawing can be made from the CAD on request.

What we need from you: a photo, the size, the material and the function of the part, the mating part if you have it, and the tolerance or fit if you know it.

### FAQ

**Can you make a spare part if there is no drawing?**
Yes. We 3D scan the part, rebuild it as an editable STEP CAD model and check the model against the scan, so you can machine, cast or print the part from the CAD.

**Can you 3D scan an old or obsolete part?**
Yes. Old, worn and broken parts can be scanned. For a worn part we rebuild the intended shape, and we agree fits and tolerances with you.

**What do I need to send?**
A photo, the approximate size, the material if you know it and what the part does. The mating part or the assembly it fits into helps a lot. Small parts can be sent to our Coimbatore facility; larger parts are scanned where they stand.

**How accurate is the rebuilt part?**
It depends on the scanner, the part and the tolerance agreed. The JINUOSH Vertex scanners we distribute are specified by the maker at up to 0.020 mm. We confirm the expected accuracy for your part before we start, and the deviation report shows how the CAD sits on the scan.

**Do you identify the material?**
We capture the shape and dimensions. We do not certify the material; if you know it, we note it with the model.

**What files will I get?**
Editable STEP (IGES or Parasolid on request), the STL or PLY mesh, and a PDF deviation report if you want one. They open in your own CAD software.

**Can you make a 2D drawing from the scan?**
Yes, on request, made from the CAD model.

**How much does it cost to rebuild a part without a drawing?**
It depends on the size, complexity, tolerance, output and location, so we quote per job. Send a photo and a short description and we reply with a written quote.
