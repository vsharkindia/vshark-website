### FAQ
**Do you have an office in Bengaluru?**
No. We serve Bengaluru from Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Parts come to us by courier, or ask us about on-site options.

**What is the difference between 3D scanning and CMM inspection?**
3D scanning captures the whole surface as a mesh; a portable CMM arm measures chosen points and features. We offer both and pick the method after seeing the part.

**What does an inspection report contain?**
A PDF with a deviation colour map, dimensions and GD&T callouts. It is normally delivered in 2 to 3 days.

**Can you visit a Bengaluru site?**
Ask us about on-site options. Send a photo of the part in place and its size, and we reply on whether a visit suits the job.

**Can you build CAD from a scan?**
Yes. We rebuild an editable STEP model and check it against the scan.

**How accurate is the scanner?**
The JINUOSH Vertex scanners we distribute are specified by the maker at up to 0.020 mm (Vertex3D and Vertex3D Pro) and 0.025 mm (Vertex Tracker). That is a maker specification, not a guarantee for every part.

**What does it cost?**
We quote per job in writing. There is no public price list.

**How do I send the part?**
Send a photo and the size first. We confirm the route, then you courier the part to Coimbatore with its mating parts.
