### How a mould or die job runs
1. **Brief.** You send a photo, the approximate size, the tool type, and what you want: CAD, an inspection or both. A sample part from the tool helps.
2. **3D scan.** Handheld blue-laser scanning suits small to medium cavities, cores, inserts and tooling; optical-tracking scanning suits large tools. We scan at our Coimbatore facility or where the tool stands. Shiny or polished mould steel can change the method (a thin matt coating may be needed), so tell us the surface. For how we handle reflective surfaces, read [how to 3D scan shiny, reflective and dark parts](/blog/scanning-shiny-reflective-parts?utm_source=website&utm_medium=seo&utm_campaign=2026-11-s32&utm_content=mould-shiny).
3. **Mesh cleaning.** The scan is merged, cleaned and checked for gaps. The mesh can be delivered as STL or PLY.
4. **CAD rebuild.** We rebuild the tool surfaces as clean, editable CAD from sections and features, not as a copy of the rough mesh. Fits, tolerances and parting-line features are agreed with you before work starts; we do not promise a tolerance until we have seen the tool and its job.
5. **Check against the scan.** The finished CAD is compared with the scan and shown as a deviation colour map. Inspection reports are normally delivered in 2 to 3 days.
6. **Delivery.** STEP (IGES or Parasolid on request) that opens in your own CAD software, plus the mesh and the deviation report if you want them.

Shrinkage compensation, draft changes and tool design stay with your tool room. We supply the as-scanned geometry, the CAD and the inspection data.

### Scan, CMM arm or both
For a freeform cavity, a core or a whole die face, a 3D scan captures the full surface. For datum-based features, bores, pin holes and tight dimensions, a portable CMM arm is the better tool, and some tool checks suit both. See our [CMM inspection services](/cmm-inspection-services?utm_source=website&utm_medium=seo&utm_campaign=2026-11-s32&utm_content=mould-cmm).

### Why scan first
A scanner captures every curve of a cavity or die face, including freeform shapes that are hard to measure by hand, and it shows wear across the whole surface instead of at a few probe points. The JINUOSH Vertex handheld blue-laser scanners we distribute are specified by the maker at up to 0.020 mm (Vertex3D and Vertex3D Pro) and 0.025 mm (Vertex Tracker). That is a scanner specification, not a guarantee for every tool; we tell you the expected accuracy for your tool before we start. ### FAQ
**Can you 3D scan a mould or die and make CAD from it?**
Yes. We 3D scan the cavity, core or die face, rebuild it as an editable STEP CAD model and check the model against the scan, so your tool room can machine a new insert or tool from the CAD.

**Can you inspect a worn mould or die?**
Yes. We scan the tool and compare it with the original CAD or a good master. The result is a deviation colour map that shows where the surface has worn or moved. What to repair, and when, stays your decision.

**Can you do first-article inspection of a moulded or cast part?**
Yes. We compare the sample with its CAD and send a PDF report with a deviation colour map, dimensions and GD&T callouts. Reports are normally delivered in 2 to 3 days.

**What kinds of tools can you scan?**
Injection moulds, die-casting dies, press tools and stamping dies, forging dies, blow and rotational moulds, patterns and core boxes. Send a photo and we tell you the best method.

**Can you scan shiny or polished mould steel?**
Usually yes, but a polished or very reflective surface can change the method, and a thin matt coating may be needed. Tell us the surface when you ask for a quote.

**Do you design or make the mould?**
No. We supply the scan data, the CAD and the inspection report. Shrinkage compensation, tool design and manufacture stay with your tool room or toolmaker.

**How accurate is the scan?**
It depends on the scanner, the tool and the tolerance agreed. The JINUOSH Vertex scanners we distribute are specified by the maker at up to 0.020 mm. We confirm the expected accuracy for your tool before we start.

**How much does mould and die scanning cost?**
It depends on the size, complexity, tolerance, output and location, so we quote per job. Send a photo and a short description and we reply with a written quote.
