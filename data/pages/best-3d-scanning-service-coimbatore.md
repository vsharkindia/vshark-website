### 8 checks before you pick a 3D scanning service in Coimbatore

1. **What are you scanning, and how big is it?** A small machined part, a casting, a vehicle body and a plant room need different scanners. Ask which equipment will be used for your size and surface (shiny, dark or black parts may need a different method).
2. **What accuracy do you need?** Reverse engineering a worn spare needs less than checking a fit-critical tolerance. Ask what accuracy the equipment is rated for, and how it is verified. Scanner ratings describe the device; the final result also depends on the part, the setup and the cleanup.
3. **What do you receive?** A mesh (STL, PLY) is enough for printing and visual checks. Manufacturing and redesign need editable CAD (STEP, IGES, Parasolid). Inspection needs a report. Ask for the formats in writing.
4. **Is an inspection report included?** If you are comparing a made part against CAD, ask for a deviation colour map and dimensions. Ours is a PDF with a deviation colour map, dimensions and GD&T callouts.
5. **Can the work be done at your plant?** Heavy dies, large castings and fixed machines are easier to scan where they stand. Ask about travel and accommodation charges before you book.
6. **How long will it take?** Scanning time depends on size and cleanup, so it is quoted per job. Our inspection reports are normally delivered in 2 to 3 days.
7. **How is the price built?** Most providers quote per hour or per job. Ask what the hour includes (scanning only, cleanup, CAD, travel) and how many revisions are included.
8. **Can they show similar work?** Ask for examples of the same kind of part. Only trust samples the provider is allowed to show.

Hire a service if you need a few scans a year. Buy a scanner if you scan every week and have a trained operator.
