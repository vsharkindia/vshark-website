### What you get
- CAD (STEP) of the mating surfaces and mount points of your vehicle zone.
- A mount-point sheet: hole positions, bolt sizes you tell us or we measure, and datum references.
- A fit-check report: your bracket or part model against the scan, with a deviation colour map and a list of clashes and gaps.
- Flat patterns (DXF) of brackets and plates for laser cutting where your part needs them.

### Who is this for?
- Makers of bumpers, bonnet scoops, fender flares, roof racks, side steps and body kits.
- Roll-cage and tube-chassis fabricators who need the cabin shape before they bend tube.
- Custom bike builders and bike-accessory makers (panniers, saddle stays, handle guards, mounts).

- Fitment shops and tuners who build one-off parts and want to repeat them.

### The problems we hear, in your words
- **"The mount points do not line up."** Holes are a few millimetres off, the bracket needs filing, and the second car is different from the first.
- **"The sensor and camera cut-outs are off."** The bumper fits, but the parking sensor, fog lamp or camera sits behind a rib.
- **"The body kit does not fit properly."** Gaps at the panel edge, a flare that rocks, a scoop that lifts at one corner.
- **"Tolerances stack up."** Each small error is fine alone. Together the part is well out at the far end.
- **"Every variant is different."** A facelift, a new trim or a different batch changes a few points, and the whole job restarts.
- **"There is no OEM drawing."** Nobody gives you the body data. You measure with tape, cardboard and trial fits, and burn sheet metal doing it.

### How it works
1. **Send the vehicle details.** Model, year, variant, and what you want to fit. Photos of the mounting area help us say what a scan can reach.
2. **We confirm what can be captured.** Tight gaps, deep recesses, dark glossy or black glass areas and underbody zones may need preparation or may not be fully captured. We tell you before we start.
3. **Scan the actual vehicle.** The whole body, or only the zone you are fitting: nose, tail, roof, wheel arches, cabin. The vehicle comes to us, or ask us about on-site options.
4. **Build the body and mount-point CAD.** We rebuild the surfaces and the fixing points as a clean model, with datums you can use. Hidden features the scan cannot reach are listed so you can check them on the car.
5. **Design or check your part.** You design your bracket or part against this CAD, or send us your sketch and we model it. We run the clearances for sensors, panel gaps and moving parts, and the fit-check report shows the result.
6. **Deviation check on your prototype.** Scan your first prototype and compare it to the design and the body. Fix the model, not the metal.
7. **Deliver, then you make.** You get the files and your shop makes the part. We do not manufacture or fit the accessory.

### What we need from you
- Vehicle model, year and variant (and market, if it is a different trim for the same model).
- Access to the vehicle for scanning, and the zone you care about.
- What you want to fit, with a sketch, a draft model or photos of the accessory.
- Bolt sizes, material and thickness of your bracket plate, if you know them.
- Any parts that must keep clear: sensors, cameras, lamps, tow points, hinges, bonnet and boot movement.
- How many parts you plan to make, and whether other variants follow.

### What we can and cannot do
**We can:**
- Scan the real vehicle, or the zone you are fitting, and rebuild its surfaces and mount points as CAD.
- Check your bracket, cut-out or accessory model against the scan, and your prototype against both.
- Produce flat DXF patterns for brackets and plates, and a mount-point sheet.
- Do this for a car, a bike or a cabin shell.

**We cannot, or will not claim to:**
- Capture every surface. Deep recesses, very tight gaps, black or glossy glass and underbody areas can be missed or need preparation. We list what was not captured.
- Know the hidden structure behind a panel. A scan sees the outside. Reinforcements, wiring and the strength of a mount point are checked on the vehicle by you.
- Certify strength or safety. Structural and safety checks, testing and certification of your accessory are yours to do.
- Make any claim about legality. Rules for modifying a vehicle differ by state and country, and we do not advise on them or state that a part meets any rule.
- Make the part, or fit it to the vehicle. Your shop does that.
- Promise a tolerance for a whole vehicle. We agree what matters for your part and report what we measured.

### FAQ
**My body kit or accessory does not fit properly. Can a scan help?**
Yes. We scan the actual vehicle and give you CAD of the mating surfaces and mount points, so you can see where your part is off and redesign it against the real body.

**What do I get at the end?**
A STEP file of the body surfaces and mount points, a mount-point sheet, and a fit-check report with a deviation map and a list of clashes and gaps. DXF flat patterns for brackets and plates are included where relevant.

**Do you scan the whole vehicle or only one zone?**
Either. Often only the zone you are fitting is needed. Tell us what you want to fit and we say which zone to scan.

**Can you check the sensor, camera and lamp cut-outs?**
Yes, if the part is in the scanned zone. We check the clearance of the cut-out against the scan and report the gap. Anything the scan cannot reach is listed so you can check it on the car.

**Do you handle different variants and facelifts?**
Each variant is scanned or checked separately, because mount points and surfaces can change. Send the variant and market, and we say what needs a new scan.

**Do you make the bumper, scoop or bracket as well?**
No. We give you the data and a fit check. Your shop makes and fits the part. We can model your bracket from your sketch and check your prototype against the data.

**Will you certify the part or say it is legal to fit?**
No. Strength, safety testing, certification and the rules for modifying a vehicle are your responsibility and differ by state and country. We give geometry and a fit check, not a legal opinion.

**How much does it cost, and how long does it take?**
It depends on the vehicle zone, the complexity of the part and where the scanning is done, so we quote per job. Send the model, year and what you want to fit and we reply with a written quote and a time after seeing your brief.
