### FAQ

**What is an optical-tracking 3D scanner?**
It is a scanner whose position is followed by a separate tracking bar. Because the bar knows where the scanner is, you can capture large parts with less reliance on stuck-on markers.

**How accurate is Vertex Tracker?**
The JINUOSH specification gives a highest precision of up to 0.025 mm, with a maximum resolution of 0.01 mm. Real-job accuracy also depends on the part, its surface and the setup.

**How big a part can Vertex Tracker scan?**
The recommended sample size is 10 mm to 8000 mm, and the maximum scanning field per capture is 1100 x 800 mm.
