### The short answer
A small part that needs only an STL mesh is a lighter job than a large cast housing that needs an editable STEP model and a report. Both are "3D scanning", but the time, the equipment and the skill differ, so the cost differs. This page lists what moves the quote so you can judge your own job before you ask.

### What decides the cost of 3D scanning
| Driver | Why it changes the quote |
|---|---|
| Part size | A bigger part needs more scanning time and more data to process. Large parts, heavy dies and fixed machines are usually scanned where they stand. |
| Complexity | Freeform surfaces, deep holes, thin walls and fine detail take longer to capture and to clean. |
| Accuracy and tolerance | The tighter the tolerance you need, the more careful the set-up and checking. We confirm the expected accuracy for your part before we start. |
| Surface | Shiny, dark or transparent surfaces can change the method and add preparation. Tell us the material and finish. |
| Output | A mesh (STL, PLY) is quicker than a full CAD model. A STEP model rebuilt from sections and features takes more work than a mesh. An inspection report adds the comparison and the PDF. |
| Scan only, scan to CAD or inspection | Scanning, reverse engineering (scan to CAD) and inspection are different jobs with different effort. See the [3D scanning services](/3d-scanning-services-india?utm_source=website&utm_medium=seo&utm_campaign=2026-11-s17&utm_content=cost-services) we offer. |
| Number of parts | Several parts of the same kind, scanned in one visit, are planned differently from a single part. |
| Turnaround | Inspection reports are normally delivered in 2 to 3 days. If you need a job faster than usual, say so when you ask. |
| File formats | STL or PLY mesh, STEP, IGES or Parasolid CAD, PDF report, or point cloud for buildings. The more finished the file, the more work behind it. |
| Location and travel | Work at our Coimbatore facility is quoted without travel. On-site work adds travel and accommodation, which are quoted separately. |
| Extras | A portable CMM arm for datum-based features or tight dimensions, or a 3D print of the scanned part, are quoted with the job (see [CMM inspection services](/cmm-inspection-services?utm_source=website&utm_medium=seo&utm_campaign=2026-11-s17&utm_content=cost-cmm)). |

### Why we quote each job instead of listing prices
A bracket and a car body, a clean machined face and a worn casting, a mesh for reference and a CAD model for production: the same words "3D scan" hide very different amounts of work. A single table of rates cannot show what your job involves. So we look at your part first and give you a written quote for that job. You see the scope, the files you get and the cost before work starts.

- Your drawing or CAD file if you have one (for an inspection, the CAD to compare with).
- The tolerance or fit that matters, if you know it.
- How many parts, and where they are (our Coimbatore facility or your site; the city).

### How to keep the cost sensible
- Tell us the output you actually need. If a mesh is enough, you do not need a full CAD model.
- Send a drawing or CAD if you have one; it shortens the CAD and inspection work.
- Group parts of the same kind into one enquiry.
- Say early if the part is shiny, dark or very large, so we plan the method.
- Say if an on-site visit can cover more than one part at your plant.

### FAQ
**How much does 3D scanning cost in India?**
It depends on the part size and complexity, the accuracy needed, the surface, the output (mesh, CAD model or report), the number of parts and the location, so we quote each job in writing. Send a photo and a short description and we reply with a written quote.

**How much does 3D scanning cost in Coimbatore?**
The same drivers apply in Coimbatore. Work at our Coimbatore facility has no travel cost; on-site work elsewhere adds travel and accommodation, quoted separately. Send a photo for a written quote.

**Why don't you publish a price list?**
Every part is different, and the same words "3D scan" cover very different amounts of work. We look at your part first and give a written quote that states the scope and the files you receive.

**What makes a 3D scanning job cost more?**
A larger or more complex part, a tighter tolerance, a shiny or dark surface, a full CAD model instead of a mesh, several parts, a short deadline, and on-site work with travel and accommodation.

**Is a scan cheaper than scan to CAD?**
A mesh output is less work than a rebuilt, editable STEP model, so it is quoted as a lighter job. What you need depends on what you will do with the file, so tell us the use and we suggest the output.

**What should I send to get an exact quote?**
Two or three clear photos, the approximate size and material, the output you want, any drawing or CAD, the tolerance if you know it, the number of parts, the location and your deadline.

**How long does it take to get a quote?**
We reply as soon as we can. A complete enquiry with photos and details lets us quote faster, and fewer details can mean questions back to you first.

**How long does an inspection report take?**
Inspection reports are normally delivered in 2 to 3 days.
