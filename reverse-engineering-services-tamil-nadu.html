Reverse Engineering Services in Tamil Nadu

VShark Solutions Pvt Ltd provides reverse engineering services across Tamil Nadu from Coimbatore. We 3D scan your part, rebuild it as an editable STEP CAD model, check the model against the scan and deliver files that open in your own CAD software. We work at our facility or at your plant. Send a photo and a short description of your part for a written quote.

When Tamil Nadu plants need reverse engineering

  • The drawing or CAD file is lost, or never existed.
  • The supplier has stopped making a spare part. See spare part with no drawing: how we rebuild it.
  • A mould, die, casting or fixture has no data and must be repeated or repaired.
  • A worn or damaged part needs its original shape back.
  • You want to compare a made part with its design, or redesign an existing part.

How a job works, in short

We brief, quote in writing, 3D scan, rebuild an editable CAD model and check it against the scan. Small parts come to our Coimbatore facility; larger parts, heavy dies and fixed machines are scanned at your plant. Inspection reports are normally delivered in 2 to 3 days. For the full workflow, deliverables, accuracy and cost drivers, see our hub page, reverse engineering services in India. For the maker's accuracy figures, see the JINUOSH Vertex scanners.

FAQ

Common questions.

Who can reverse engineer a part for me in Tamil Nadu?

VShark Solutions Pvt Ltd in Coimbatore, scanning at our facility or at your plant. We provide reverse engineering, 3D scanning, scan to CAD and inspection for manufacturers and tool rooms across Tamil Nadu.

How do I send a part to you from a Tamil Nadu town?

Send a photo, the approximate size and what the part does first. For a small part we then confirm that you can courier or post it to our Coimbatore facility, and you send the part with notes on the output you need.

Should you scan on site or should I send the part?

Small parts can be sent to us. Larger parts, heavy dies and fixed machines are scanned at your plant. Travel and accommodation for site work are quoted separately.

What should my first photo show?

The whole part, a close view of the area you care about, and the surface, because shiny or dark surfaces and deep holes can change the scanning method. Add the approximate size and the output you need, such as STEP CAD.

What files do I receive?

An editable STEP model that opens in your own CAD software, and on request IGES or Parasolid, the mesh and a deviation report.

How long does an inspection report take?

Inspection reports are normally delivered in 2 to 3 days.

Do you have an office in Chennai, Madurai, Salem or the other towns?

No. We are based in Coimbatore and serve Chennai, Madurai, Tiruppur, Salem, Erode, Hosur and Trichy from there.
