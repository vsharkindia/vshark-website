3D Printing From Your 3D Scan in India

VShark Solutions Pvt Ltd, Coimbatore, starts with a 3D scan of your part. We clean the scan into a printable model and print it in FDM or SLA, as a copy, a fit-check or a prototype. If the part has to be changed or machined later, we rebuild the scan as editable STEP CAD before printing. We work at our Coimbatore facility or at your site across India. Every job here begins with a scan, so no drawing is needed. We can scan mould inserts, broken parts, samples and hand-made masters. We tell you if a part is not suited to printing before you pay for it.

How a job runs: scan, clean, optional CAD, print

  1. Brief. You send a photo, the approximate size, what the part must do and how many you need.
  2. 3D scan. We scan the part at our Coimbatore facility or visit your site, so a heavy or fixed part need not be moved. Very shiny or dark surfaces can change the method, so tell us the surface.
  3. Mesh clean-up. The scan is merged, gaps are closed and the mesh is made watertight so a printer can read it. We scale or edit it if you ask.
  4. Optional CAD. If the part will be modified or machined later, we rebuild the scan as clean, editable STEP CAD (IGES or Parasolid on request) from sections and features, not as a copy of the rough mesh.
  5. You approve the model. Nothing is printed until you have seen and agreed the model.
  6. Print. We print it in FDM or SLA and hand it over. The print-ready file is available on request.

Materials, build size and delivery time depend on the scan, the part and your preference, so we confirm them for each job.

FDM or SLA?

FDM suits larger, strong functional parts and quick checks. SLA suits fine detail and smooth surfaces on smaller parts. Tell us the part size and what it must do, and we recommend one.

Checking the scan before you print

A print is only as good as the scan under it. If a mating fit matters, tell us at the start so the scan and the model are checked for that fit. For a part that must meet a tolerance, we can also compare the scan with CAD as a deviation colour map, or measure with a portable CMM arm. See our CMM inspection services.

What decides the cost

We quote per job and do not publish a price list. The main drivers are:

  • the size of the part and how many you need,
  • how complex the shape is and how much mesh repair it needs,
  • whether CAD is needed before printing,
  • FDM or SLA, and the material you choose,
  • on-site scanning, travel and accommodation.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can you 3D print something that exists only as a physical part?

Yes. We scan the part first, so no drawing is needed. We then clean the scan into a printable model and print it in FDM or SLA.

Do you print with FDM or SLA?

Both. We pick the process from the size, detail and strength you need. FDM suits larger, strong functional parts and quick checks. SLA suits fine detail and smooth surfaces on smaller parts.

Is the printed part the same size as the original?

We print at the scale you ask, 1:1 or scaled. If a mating fit matters, tell us at the start so we check it. We do not promise a printed tolerance until we have seen the part.

Can you make CAD from the scan before printing?

Yes. If the part must be modified or machined later, we rebuild the scan as an editable STEP model, then print from it. Ask for IGES or Parasolid if you need them.

Can I get the print-ready file only?

Yes. Ask for the model file when you send the enquiry.

Can you print small batches?

Yes, for one-off parts and small runs. Send the quantity and a photo for a quote.

What materials can you use?

Materials depend on the scan, the part and your preference, so we confirm them for each job. Tell us what the part must do and we recommend an FDM or SLA material.

How much does 3D printing from a scan cost?

It depends on the size, complexity, CAD needed, process, material and location, so we quote per job. Send a photo and a short description and we reply with a written quote.
