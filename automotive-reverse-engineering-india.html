Automotive 3D Scanning and Reverse Engineering in India

VShark Solutions Pvt Ltd, Coimbatore, 3D scans automotive parts and rebuilds them as editable STEP CAD models. We also inspect parts and tooling against CAD or a master part and report the difference as a deviation colour map. Typical jobs are aftermarket parts, tooling and fixtures, body panels, interior trims and worn or obsolete vehicle parts. We work at our Coimbatore facility or at your site across India. How an automotive job runs

  1. Brief. You send a photo, the approximate size, the material if known, the vehicle or assembly it fits, and what you want: CAD, an inspection or both. The mating part helps.
  2. 3D scan. Handheld blue-laser scanning suits small to medium parts, castings and tooling; optical-tracking scanning suits large parts such as body sections. We scan at our Coimbatore facility or where the part or vehicle stands. Shiny, dark or glossy paint can change the method, so tell us the surface (see how to 3D scan shiny, reflective and dark parts).
  3. Mesh cleaning. The scan is merged, cleaned and checked for gaps. The mesh can be delivered as STL or PLY.
  4. CAD rebuild. We rebuild the part as clean, editable CAD from sections and features, not as a copy of the rough mesh. Class-A surfaces, fits and tolerances are agreed with you before work starts; we do not promise a tolerance until we have seen the part and its job.
  5. Check against the scan. The finished CAD is compared with the scan and shown as a deviation colour map. Inspection reports are normally delivered in 2 to 3 days.
  6. Delivery. STEP (IGES or Parasolid on request) that opens in your own CAD software, plus the mesh and the deviation report if you want them.

Scan, CMM arm or both

For a freeform surface, a casting or a whole panel, a 3D scan captures the full surface. For datum-based features, bores and tight dimensions, a portable CMM arm is the better tool, and many jobs use both. See our CMM inspection services.

What decides the cost

We quote per job and do not publish a price list. The main drivers are:

  • the size and number of parts,
  • how complex the shape and surfaces are,
  • the fit and tolerance you need,
  • the output (a mesh is quicker than a full CAD model),
  • on-site work, travel and accommodation.

Why scan first

A scanner captures every curve of a part, including freeform shapes that are hard to measure by hand. The JINUOSH Vertex handheld blue-laser scanners we distribute are specified by the maker at up to 0.020 mm (Vertex3D and Vertex3D Pro) and 0.025 mm (Vertex Tracker). That is a scanner specification, not a guarantee for every part; we tell you the expected accuracy for your part before we start. See the JINUOSH Vertex scanners.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can you 3D scan a car part and make CAD from it?

Yes. We 3D scan the part, rebuild it as an editable STEP CAD model and check the model against the scan, so you can machine, cast, mould or 3D print from the CAD.

What automotive parts can you reverse engineer?

Aftermarket and spare parts, body and exterior parts, interior trims and brackets, wheels, brake and suspension parts, castings, and tooling, jigs and fixtures. Send a photo and we tell you the best method.

Can you scan a whole vehicle or a large body part?

Yes. Large parts and vehicle sections are scanned on site with optical-tracking scanning, where the vehicle stands. Tell us the size and the surface finish.

Can you inspect automotive tooling or a first article?

Yes. We compare a part or tool with its CAD or a master part and send a PDF report with a deviation colour map, dimensions and GD&T callouts. Reports are normally delivered in 2 to 3 days.

Will the part be approved for use on a vehicle?

We supply CAD and inspection data. Approval or homologation of an aftermarket part stays with the maker and the testing body, not with us.

How accurate is the scan?

It depends on the scanner, the part and the tolerance agreed. The JINUOSH Vertex scanners we distribute are specified by the maker at up to 0.020 mm. We confirm the expected accuracy for your part before we start.

Is it legal to reverse engineer a car part?

Reverse engineering your own parts, or parts you are licensed to copy, is a normal engineering job, and the IP stays with you. For patents or protected designs, ask your own legal adviser.

How much does automotive reverse engineering cost?

It depends on the size, complexity, tolerance, output and location, so we quote per job. Send a photo and a short description and we reply with a written quote.
