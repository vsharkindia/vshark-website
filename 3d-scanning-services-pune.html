How a Pune job runs

  1. Photograph the part from three sides with a ruler beside it and send the photo with the output you need.
  2. We reply with the scanning method and a written quote.
  3. Courier the part, packed as described below, to Coimbatore.
  4. We scan it, rebuild the model or run the deviation check, and send the files by email.

Sending a part from Pune

Pack the part in a rigid box with foam, keep mating parts together, and send a photo before it moves. We confirm the route first.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you have an office in Pune?

No. We serve Pune from Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Parts come to us by courier, or ask us about on-site options.

How do I send a part from Pune for 3D scanning?

Send a photo, the approximate size and the output you need. We confirm the route, then you pack the part in a rigid box with its mating parts and courier it to Coimbatore.

Can you scan a die or machine at my Pune plant?

Ask us about on-site options. Send a photo of the part in place and its size, and we reply on whether a visit suits the job.

What do I get after the scan?

An STL or PLY mesh, an editable STEP, IGES or Parasolid model, or a PDF inspection report with a deviation colour map. Inspection reports are normally delivered in 2 to 3 days.

Can you rebuild a part that has no drawing?

Yes. We scan it, rebuild an editable CAD model and check the model against the scan. See the process in our spare part guide.

Do you also check parts against a drawing?

Yes. We offer portable CMM arm inspection and scan-based deviation reports.

What does it cost?

We quote per job in writing. The drivers are part size, surface, accuracy, output and location. There is no public price list.

Which scanners do you use?

We distribute JINUOSH Vertex handheld scanners in India and scan with the Vertex and Vertex Tracker.
