3D Scanning

Capture any part, product or space as an accurate digital model.

What we do

We scan parts of every size with a range of professional 3D scanners — from small precision components to car bodies and full buildings. The result is a precise digital copy of the physical object, ready for product design, reverse engineering and quality control.

01

High-accuracy capture

Blue-laser and structured-light scanners record detail down to hundredths of a millimetre.

02

Any size, on site or in-house

From jewellery to vehicles and plant rooms — we bring the scanner to you when the part can't travel.

03

Clean, usable data

Scans are aligned, cleaned and merged into watertight meshes, not raw point dumps.

04

Ready for the next step

Data is prepared for reverse engineering, inspection, printing or visualisation.

Deliverables
  • Point cloud (.PLY, .TXT)
  • Mesh (.STL, .OBJ)
  • Colour/texture scans
  • Scan report
