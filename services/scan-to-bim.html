Scan to BIM

Existing buildings captured as accurate, intelligent BIM models.

What we do

We laser-scan existing structures and convert the point cloud into detailed BIM models. Architects, engineers and contractors get an accurate as-built foundation for renovation, retrofit and facility-management projects — with every wall, slab, column and service documented.

01

As-built accuracy

Terrestrial laser scanning records the true geometry of the building, not the original drawings.

02

LOD 200–400 models

Walls, floors, structure and MEP modelled to the level of detail your project needs.

03

Clash-ready coordination

Models are set up for coordination with new design work in Revit and Navisworks.

04

Drawings from the model

Floor plans, sections and elevations exported straight from the BIM.

Deliverables
  • Registered point cloud (.E57, .RCP)
  • Revit model (.RVT)
  • IFC export
  • 2D plans, sections, elevations

FAQ

Scan to BIM FAQ.

Do you do the laser scanning for BIM projects?

Yes. Our team scans the building or plant room on site with terrestrial laser scanners suited to the space. If you already have a point cloud from your own survey team, we can model from that instead.

What software do you deliver BIM models in?

Autodesk Revit (RVT).

How accurate is a Scan to BIM model?

The point cloud itself is highly accurate. Model accuracy depends on the LOD and modelling tolerances agreed for the project, which we define at the scoping stage.

How do you price Scan to BIM?

By area or volume, complexity and LOD. Photos, any existing drawings and a short description of the site (or a sample of your point cloud) are enough for an initial estimate.
