3D Scanning and Reverse Engineering for UAE Companies

VShark Solutions provides 3D scanning, reverse engineering and scan to CAD for companies in the UAE, including Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi. You can ship the part to our Coimbatore, India workshop, send your own scan data, or ask for on-site scanning in the UAE. Send a photo and a short description for a written quote.

How a UAE job runs

  1. Send photos, sizes, material and the output you need (email or WhatsApp).
  2. We confirm what can be done from photos or your scan data, what needs the part shipped, and what we can scan on-site.
  3. If the part must move, we agree the shipping route and paperwork with you first.
  4. We scan and build the model, then check it against the scan.
  5. You receive the files by email, with a written quote agreed before work starts.

Sending a part from the UAE

You can ship parts to our Coimbatore workshop in India. Send a photo first; we confirm what can move and agree the shipping route with you before anything is sent. Many jobs start from photos, drawings or your own scan data and need no shipping at all.

Remote scan to CAD

If your team has a scanner, send the mesh (STL, PLY or OBJ). We rebuild it as an editable model and return STEP, IGES or Parasolid. Working hours are 9 AM to 7 PM IST, which is 7:30 AM to 5:30 PM UAE time.

On-site scanning in the UAE

We offer on-site 3D scanning in the UAE. Send a photo of the part in place, its size and the output you need, and we confirm the visit and quote it in writing.

What you receive

  • STL or PLY mesh
  • Editable STEP, IGES or Parasolid model
  • PDF inspection report with a deviation colour map
  • Most reports and models are ready 2 to 3 days after scanning

FAQ

Common questions.

Where do you work from for UAE customers?

We serve the UAE (Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi) in two ways: you ship parts to our workshop in Coimbatore, India, or we scan on-site in the UAE.

Can you work on parts from Dubai, Sharjah or Abu Dhabi?

Yes. Send a photo, the size and the output you need. We confirm what can be done from photos or your own scan data, what needs the physical part shipped to Coimbatore, and what we can scan on-site in the UAE.

Can you do scan to CAD remotely?

Yes. If you ship a part or send your own scan data, we build and check an editable STEP model and send the files by email.

Do you scan on-site in the UAE?
