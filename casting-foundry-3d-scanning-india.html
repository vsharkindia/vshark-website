How a casting job runs

  1. Brief. You send a photo, the approximate size and weight, the material and what you want: an inspection, CAD or both. Send the casting CAD and drawing if you have them.
  2. 3D scan. Handheld blue-laser scanning suits small to medium castings and patterns; optical-tracking scanning suits large castings and patterns. We scan at our Coimbatore facility or where the casting stands, so heavy items need not be moved. Very shiny machined faces can change the method, so tell us the surface.
  3. Mesh cleaning. The scan is merged, cleaned and checked for gaps. The mesh can be delivered as STL or PLY.
  4. Compare or rebuild. For inspection, the scan is aligned to your CAD and shown as a deviation colour map. For a part with no CAD, we rebuild the surfaces as clean, editable CAD from sections and features, not as a copy of the rough mesh. We do not promise a tolerance until we have seen the casting and its job.
  5. Report. Inspection reports are normally delivered in 2 to 3 days.
  6. Delivery. A PDF report, plus STEP (IGES or Parasolid on request) and the mesh if you want them.

Shrinkage allowance, gating and pattern design stay with your foundry or pattern shop. We supply the scan data, the CAD and the inspection report.

Scan, CMM arm or both

A cast surface rarely sits exactly where the drawing puts it. A 3D scan covers the whole casting, so a thin wall, core shift or parting-line mismatch shows in colour, not only at a few probe points. For datum-based features, bores and tight machined dimensions, a portable CMM arm is the better tool, and some checks suit both. See our CMM inspection services.

What decides the cost

We quote per job and do not publish a price list. The main drivers are:

  • the size, weight and number of castings or patterns,
  • how complex the shape is,
  • the tolerance or allowance you work to,
  • the output (a colour map is quicker than a full CAD model),
  • on-site work, travel and accommodation.

Why scan first

A scanner captures the whole casting, including freeform surfaces that are hard to measure by hand, and shows variation across the surface instead of at a few points. The JINUOSH Vertex handheld blue-laser scanners we distribute are specified by the maker at up to 0.020 mm (Vertex3D and Vertex3D Pro) and 0.025 mm (Vertex Tracker). That is a scanner specification, not a guarantee for every casting; we tell you the expected accuracy for your part before we start. See the JINUOSH Vertex scanners.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can you 3D scan a casting and check it against CAD?

Yes. We scan the casting, align it to your CAD and send a PDF report with a deviation colour map, dimensions and GD&T callouts. Reports are normally delivered in 2 to 3 days.

Can you check machining allowance on a rough casting?

Yes. We align the scan of the rough casting to the finished-part CAD, and the colour map shows the stock left on each face, including any face that would clean up short.

Do you inspect patterns and core boxes?

Yes. We scan a wooden or metal pattern or core box and compare it with its CAD, so worn corners, damaged core prints and allowance errors can be corrected before the next mould is made.

Can you make CAD of a pattern or casting that has no drawing?

Yes. We scan the item and rebuild it as an editable STEP model, then check the model against the scan. Shrinkage allowance and pattern design stay with your pattern shop or foundry.

Can you come to our foundry?

Yes. We scan at your plant anywhere in India, so heavy castings and large patterns need not be moved. Travel and accommodation are quoted with the job.

What castings can you scan?

Sand, die, investment and gravity castings, forgings, patterns, core boxes and cast wear parts. Send a photo and we tell you the best method.

How accurate is the scan?

It depends on the scanner, the casting and the tolerance agreed. The JINUOSH Vertex scanners we distribute are specified by the maker at up to 0.020 mm. We confirm the expected accuracy for your casting before we start.

How much does casting scanning and inspection cost?

It depends on the size, complexity, tolerance, output and location, so we quote per job. Send a photo and a short description and we reply with a written quote.
