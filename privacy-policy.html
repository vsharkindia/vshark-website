Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

VShark Solutions Private Limited ("VShark", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use www.vsharksolutions.com (the "Website") or contact us, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Contact for privacy matters: info@vsharksolutions.com
Address: VShark Solutions Private Limited, Rajiv Gandhi Nagar, Neelambur, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu 641062, India

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiry details: your name, company, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, the service or product you're interested in, and your project description
  • Files you upload or send: drawings, photos, CAD models, scan data or other documents you share with your enquiry, by email or on WhatsApp
  • Communications: messages you send us by email, phone or WhatsApp

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages visited, time on site, referring website, device and browser type, approximate location (country or city) and IP address
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see Section 6

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations and deliver the services or products you request
  • review the files you send so we can assess and carry out your project
  • communicate with you about your project, order, delivery, installation, training or warranty
  • improve our Website and services, and understand how visitors use the Website
  • measure the effectiveness of our advertising, for example Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google ads
  • meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

We process your data based on your consent (for example when you submit the contact form), to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, to perform a contract with you, and to meet our legal obligations.

3. Your files and confidential information

We understand that drawings, CAD models and scan data are often confidential.

  • Files you send are used only to assess and deliver your project.
  • We do not publish or share your files or project details without your permission.
  • We are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) before you share files.
  • Project images appear in our portfolio or marketing only with the client's approval.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as website hosting, email, cloud storage, analytics and advertising platforms (for example Google and Meta), under appropriate confidentiality and data-protection terms
  • Our technology partner JINUOSH, only when needed to supply, register or support a scanner you purchase (for example warranty registration)
  • Authorities, when required by law

Some service providers may store data outside India or the UAE. When this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your data stays protected.

5. How long we keep your information

  • Enquiries that don't lead to a project: up to 24 months, then deleted
  • Project files and records: for the duration of the project and as long as reasonably needed for support, repeat orders or legal and tax requirements
  • You can ask us to delete your data earlier (see Section 8).

6. Cookies and analytics

The Website uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • make the Website work properly (essential cookies)
  • understand how visitors use the Website (Google Analytics)
  • measure and improve our advertising (Meta Pixel, Google Ads)

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the Website works.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including secure (HTTPS) connections, access controls and trusted service providers. No online transmission is completely secure, so please avoid sending highly sensitive information unless needed. For very sensitive files, ask us about a secure transfer method.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the UAE's Personal Data Protection Law, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • correct or update inaccurate data
  • ask us to delete your data
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this doesn't affect processing already carried out)
  • raise a grievance with us, and, if unresolved, with the relevant data-protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email info@vsharksolutions.com with the subject "Privacy request". We will respond within a reasonable time and as required by law.

9. Children

Our Website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18.

10. Third-party links

The Website may link to other websites, such as social media, WhatsApp and our partners. We are not responsible for their privacy practices; please read their policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the "Last updated" date.
