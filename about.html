( 05 ) — About

We capture reality. Then we engineer it.

VShark Solutions Private Limited is a 3D scanning and engineering design company based in Coimbatore, India, with a presence in Dubai, UAE. Since 2019, we have helped manufacturers, engineers, designers and builders turn physical objects into accurate, usable digital data, and turn digital designs back into real parts.

Our clients range from automotive and aerospace suppliers to foundries, mining and process plants, construction teams, jewellers and research labs. We have delivered projects for clients in India, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, the USA, the UK, Germany and Canada.

What we do

Seven services, one team.

  • 3D ScanningHigh-accuracy blue-laser scanning of parts, castings, vehicles and large objects, at our facility or on site anywhere in India and the UAE.
  • Reverse EngineeringWorn, legacy and undocumented parts rebuilt as editable, manufacturable CAD, delivered as STEP and other neutral formats.
  • Scan to BIMBuildings and plant rooms laser-scanned on site, or your own point cloud, modelled as accurate Autodesk Revit BIM.
  • Class A SurfacingSmooth, curvature-continuous surfaces for visible automotive and product parts.
  • Digital MetrologyScan-to-CAD inspection with deviation colour maps and clear reports.
  • 3D PrintingFDM and SLA prototypes, functional parts, scale models and jewellery masters.
  • Product RenderingPhotorealistic visuals of products before they are made.

Five steps, proven results.

  2. 02Capture accuratelyThe right scanner and setup for the part's size, surface and tolerance.
