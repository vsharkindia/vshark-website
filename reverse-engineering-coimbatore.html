What to bring or send

  • The part itself, and any mating part it fits with.
  • Any drawing, old CAD file, photo or sample, even if it is only partly right.
  • A note on what the part does, where it wears or fails, and the output you need (editable STEP CAD, a mesh, or an inspection report).
  • The surface condition: shiny, dark, oily or coated surfaces and deep holes can change the scanning method. Tell us before you bring it.

Who provides reverse engineering services in Coimbatore?

Can I bring my part to your facility?

Yes. Contact us first by email or the form, send a photo of the part, and we confirm a time and the scanning method before you travel.

Can you come to my plant in Coimbatore?

Yes. Large parts, heavy dies and machines that cannot be moved are scanned where they stand. Send a photo of the part in place, the approximate size and the surface, and we agree a visit time.

What should I bring or send?

The part and any mating part, any drawing, old CAD file or sample, a note on what the part does and where it fails, the output you need, and the surface condition (shiny, dark, oily or deep holes).

What files do I receive, and how long does an inspection report take?

An editable STEP model that opens in your own CAD software, and on request IGES or Parasolid, the mesh and a deviation report. Inspection reports are normally delivered in 2 to 3 days.
