FAQ

Buying a scanner: FAQ.

What is the price of a 3D scanner in India?

Pricing depends on the model, software and accessories. Contact us for a current quotation for Vertex3D, Vertex3D Pro or Vertex Tracker.

Can I see the scanner working before I buy?

Yes. We offer demos on your own parts, so you can judge the data quality yourself.

Which model is best for an engineering college?

For teaching and research labs, the Vertex3D usually offers the best balance of capability and cost. It handles the wide range of student and research parts well.

What's included when I buy a scanner?

Our engineers install the scanner at your site and train your team when it's delivered. The scanner comes with a 12-month warranty.

Do you also supply scanners in the UAE?

Contact us with your location and requirements and we will advise on availability and support.
