3D Scanning Services in Coimbatore.

VShark Solutions Pvt Ltd offers 3D scanning services in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. We scan parts, moulds, castings and tooling at our facility or at your plant, then deliver an STL mesh or an editable STEP CAD model.

What we scan

If a part has no drawing, is worn, or must be checked against its CAD, we can capture it in 3D and turn the scan into data your team can use.

01

Machined parts, castings and forgings

Capture the real geometry, including wear, shrinkage and casting variation.

02

Moulds, dies and tooling

Rebuild tooling that has no drawing, or check it against its CAD.

03

Worn or broken spares

Digitise out-of-production parts so they can be remade or repaired.

04

Fixtures, jigs and assemblies

Record how an assembly really fits together.

05

Vehicle parts and body panels

Scan panels, under-body and suspension areas for design or repair work.

06

Reverse engineering, inspection and equipment

Scan to CAD, inspect, or buy the scanner.

Many buyers need a CAD model of a part that has no drawing. We turn the scan into an editable model so a tool room or foundry can make, repair or improve the part. We also offer CMM arm inspection for dimensional checks against a drawing or CAD model, and we are the India distributor of JINUOSH Vertex 3D scanners.

FAQ

3D scanning in Coimbatore FAQ.

Do you offer 3D scanning in Coimbatore?

Yes. VShark Solutions offers 3D scanning in Coimbatore, at our facility or at your plant, and delivers STL meshes or editable CAD files.

Can you scan at my factory?

Yes. We travel to your plant in Coimbatore and across India. This suits large parts, heavy castings and tooling that cannot be moved.

Can you turn a scanned part into editable CAD?

Yes. We provide reverse engineering and scan-to-CAD, delivered as STEP files and other neutral formats such as IGES and Parasolid.

How long does a 3D scanning job take?

Small parts can be scanned in under a day. Larger parts and bigger quantities take more time, and CAD modelling adds time depending on the part. We give a delivery date with the quote.

How much does 3D scanning cost in Coimbatore?

The price depends on the part size, surface, accuracy and location, so we quote per job. As of November 2026 our listed rates are Rs 1,500 per hour for scanner-based inspection and scanning, Rs 2,500 per hour for portable CMM arm work, Rs 1,000 per hour for CAD design and Rs 3,000 per hour for building scans. On-site travel and accommodation are charged extra.

What file formats do you deliver?

STL and PLY meshes, and STEP, IGES or Parasolid CAD files. Inspection results come as deviation colour maps and reports.

Do you offer CMM inspection in Coimbatore?

Yes. We offer CMM arm inspection for dimensional checks against a drawing or CAD model.
